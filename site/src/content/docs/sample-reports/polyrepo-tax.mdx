## What it shows

| Section | In this sample |
|---|---|
| Headline | $4,072 a year expected, $1,471–$4,072 depending on which bugs are counted |
| Where the money goes | Bug costs dominate; internal bumps and releases cost $270 a year between them |
| Bugs | 1 high confidence (a reverted `@acme/ui` 2.0 bump), 3 medium (a date-parsing fix copied into three repos, a fix two days after a bump, a fix shipped through a bump) |
| Internal dependency churn | 4 bumps, 3 releases, a 3-day median lag from release to bump, `@acme/ui` at v1 in `admin-app` and v2 in `web-app` |
| Migration | Migrate `ui-lib`, `web-app` and `admin-app` together; keep `docs-site` separate; align React 17 and 18 first; payback in about 23.6 months |

A sample report says "migrate" only because its history was planted to. On the public [unjs](https://github.com/unjs) repos the same skill says not to; see the [walkthrough](/guides/polyrepo-to-monorepo/#5-decide).

## Get one for your repositories

```text
Use polyrepo-tax on acme/web-app, acme/admin-app and acme/ui-lib for the last 12 months. Our loaded cost is $120/hour.
```

The report contains your repository names, commit subjects and SHAs, so share it like the repositories themselves. See [Build the business case for a monorepo](/guides/polyrepo-to-monorepo/) for the full walkthrough.
